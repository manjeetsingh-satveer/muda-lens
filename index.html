The problem

A chip spends most of its life in a fab waiting.

In our model of a 12-step power-device line, only 12% of lead time is real work. The rest is muda: queues, holds, extra inspections, long moves. The data to catch it already exists. The judgment to act on it is scarce.

Why now

01 · More fabs

New lines must ramp fast

Governments are funding new fabs in the US, Europe and Asia. Every new line has to get its yield up quickly.

02 · Fewer experts

~67,000 short by 2030

The projected US shortfall of semiconductor workers (SIA & Oxford Economics, 2023). There aren’t enough black belts to go round.

03 · Cheap decisions

A cent buys ~60 calls

Decision models like Drex answer in ~0.3 s for $0.16 per 1,000. Expert judgment can now run on every lot.
